## What is this?

Rhino MCP is a free plugin from McNeel that connects Rhino to AI assistants
like **Claude Desktop**, **Claude Code**, and **Cursor**. Once it's installed,
your assistant can see what's in your document, make geometry, run commands,
build Grasshopper definitions, and explain what it did &mdash; all from a
normal chat conversation.

You stay in control. The assistant works in your active Rhino window, and
every change shows up live so you can undo, tweak, or take over at any
point.
